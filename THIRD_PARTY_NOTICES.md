# Third-party components

Sporebound is an independently authored addon. Fungal Infection: Spore is by
Harbinger; it is a required external dependency under its upstream license.
No upstream JAR, structure NBT, texture, sound or model is embedded in this addon.
The generated structure definitions reference templates supplied by Spore at runtime.

The Gradle wrapper is supplied under the Apache License 2.0, as stated in its
launcher scripts. NeoForge and Minecraft are external development/runtime dependencies.
The Rift Talisman model references vanilla Minecraft item textures at runtime.
The HUD fungal crest is original code-drawn pixel geometry.

ConcentricWorld and Civillis are optional runtime integrations. Their binaries are
kept outside source control and the distributed addon JAR. Artifact identities are
recorded under compatibility/.
